Admissions 2026–27

Seats are open from Nursery through Grade 10. Start with an enquiry — the office follows up within one working day.

How to apply

Admission process

1

Submit enquiry

Fill the form below or visit the front office with basic details about your child.

2

Interaction

Bring the child for an informal interaction with the academic coordinator — not an exam, just a conversation.

3

Document verification

Submit required documents: birth certificate, previous report card, transfer certificate (if applicable), passport photos.

4

Confirmation

Seat confirmed on a first-eligible basis. Pay the admission fee and collect the welcome kit.

Documents required

What to bring

  • Birth certificate (original + photocopy)
  • Previous year's report card / marksheet
  • Transfer certificate from previous school (for Grade 2 and above)
  • Aadhaar card of the child (photocopy)
  • 4 passport-size photographs of the child
  • Parent/guardian ID proof (Aadhaar/PAN)
  • Address proof (utility bill / rent agreement)

Transfer certificates

Requesting a TC

To request a Transfer Certificate, visit the school office with:

  • Application letter addressed to the Principal
  • Clearance of all pending fees
  • Return of library books and school property

TCs are typically processed within 5–7 working days. For urgent requests, contact the office directly.
