Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions from parents — about admissions, timings, transport and more.

Admissions

Admission-related questions

Submit an enquiry through the "Apply Now" form or visit the front office. Bring the child for an informal interaction and document verification. Seats are confirmed on a first-eligible basis for each grade.

Admissions for the next academic session typically open in January–February. For mid-session admissions (subject to availability), contact the office directly.

Birth certificate, previous report card, transfer certificate (for Grade 2+), Aadhaar card copy, 4 passport photos, and parent/guardian ID proof. See the full list on our Admission page.

For Nursery and KG, there is only an informal interaction. For Grade 1 and above, there may be a basic assessment in English and Mathematics to gauge the child's current level — it is not eliminatory.

Fees vary by grade. An indicative fee table is available on our Admission page. Contact the office for the latest schedule and sibling discounts.

School life

Day-to-day questions

Classes run 8:00 am to 2:00 pm, Monday through Saturday. The front office is open 8:30 am to 2:00 pm on the same days, and closed Sundays.
