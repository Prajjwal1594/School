About St. Saviour School

Building character, curiosity and confidence since 2018 — from our founding in Garden Residency to one of Jaipur's trusted neighbourhood schools.

From the Principal's desk

Education is not preparation for life — it is life itself

At St. Saviour School, we believe that every child carries a unique spark. Our role is not to mould them into a single shape, but to give them the environment, the mentors, and the freedom to discover what they're capable of.

Over nearly three decades, we have stayed true to one principle: know every student by name, understand their strengths and struggles, and meet them where they are. Academic rigour matters — but so does kindness, curiosity, and the courage to try something new.

We invite you to visit our campus and see this philosophy in action.

— The Principal, St. Saviour School

Mission & Values

What we stand for

Our Mission

To provide a nurturing environment where every child develops academic competence, creative confidence, and the values needed to contribute positively to society — through dedicated teaching, small class sizes, and a curriculum that balances tradition with modern pedagogy.

Our Vision

To be Jaipur's most trusted neighbourhood school — known not for size, but for the depth of attention each student receives and the breadth of opportunity beyond the classroom.

Core Values

🎯 Diligence

Consistent effort over shortcuts — in study, in teaching, in character.

🤝 Respect

For every student, every teacher, every parent — and for the process of learning itself.

🌱 Growth

Academic, personal, creative — we measure success across all three.

💡 Curiosity

Questions are welcome. A child who asks "why" is already learning.

Our journey

Milestones along the way

2018

School founded

St. Saviour School established its campus in Garden Residency with a clear vision: quality education, strong moral values, small classes, and personal attention for every child.

2020

Smart classrooms introduced

Integrated interactive digital learning tools and smart boards across classrooms, keeping lessons engaging and concept-driven.

2022

Secondary wing & modern labs

Expanded to complete secondary schooling with dedicated Science and Computer laboratories, establishing high-standard practical learning.

2024

Holistic co-curriculars & sports

Expanded outdoor play areas, indoor activity zones, and 14 student clubs fostering leadership and talent beyond the classroom.

2026

Excellence & growing

Consistently strong Class 10 CBSE results, 65+ dedicated faculty, and a thriving community of curious, confident learners.
