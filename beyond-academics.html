Beyond Academics

14 co-curricular clubs, a full sports calendar, and dedicated arts programmes — because talents outside the syllabus deserve room to grow.

Co-curricular activities

Where interests become abilities

🎨 Art & Craft

Drawing, painting, clay modelling and paper craft — weekly studio sessions with a dedicated art teacher for all grades.

🎵 Music & Dance

Vocal and instrumental training (keyboard, tabla, guitar), plus classical and contemporary dance classes.

🎭 Theatre & Drama

Annual plays, inter-house skits, and elocution competitions — building confidence, expression and teamwork.

🧪 Science Club

Experiments, exhibitions, quiz competitions and field trips — sparking curiosity beyond the textbook.

📝 Literary Club

Debates, creative writing, poetry recitation and school magazine — honing language skills and critical thinking.

🌍 Eco Club

Environmental awareness, cleanliness drives, tree plantation and waste management — building responsible citizens.

Sports & physical education

A body in motion, a mind at its best

Physical education is part of the timetable, not an optional extra. Every student gets structured PE classes, and the school participates in inter-school and district-level competitions across multiple sports.

  • Cricket — boys and girls teams, seasonal coaching
  • Football — inter-house and inter-school matches
  • Basketball — court on campus, regular practice
  • Athletics — annual sports day with track and field events
  • Table Tennis & Chess — indoor games room
  • Yoga & PT — daily morning exercises for all grades
  • Kabaddi & Kho-Kho — traditional sports tournaments
