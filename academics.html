Academics

A structured curriculum from Nursery to Grade 10 — designed to build strong foundations and prepare students for board exams and beyond.

Academic wings

One campus, three stages of growth

Pre-Primary Wing

Grades · Nursery & KGHours · 8:30 am–12:30 pm

A caring, stimulating environment focused on sensory exploration, curiosity, phonetic play, and social habits so children develop a natural love for learning.

  • Play-way & activity-based foundational learning
  • Phonetics, basic numbers and storytelling
  • Motor skill exercises and creative free play
  • Colourful, safe and child-friendly play zone

Primary Wing

Grades · 1–5Hours · 8:00 am–2:00 pm

Reading, writing and arithmetic form the solid base — taught through interactive modules and concept-building exercises alongside art, computers, and physical education.

  • English, Hindi and Mathematics core proficiency
  • Environmental Studies (EVS) and General Knowledge
  • Hands-on computer awareness and digital literacy
  • Music, art, yoga and physical education

Secondary Wing

Grades · 6–10Hours · 8:00 am–2:00 pm

Scholastic rigor and analytical thinking take centre stage, guiding students smoothly through middle school and preparing them thoroughly for the CBSE Class 10 Board Examinations.

  • Comprehensive CBSE Class 10 curriculum & practicals
  • Dedicated Science and Computer Laboratories
  • Regular unit assessments, remedial support & mock exams
  • Leadership development, sports, and house competitions
Curriculum

How we teach

📝 Continuous assessment

Regular class tests, unit tests and term exams — not just one final paper — so gaps are caught early.

🔬 Hands-on practicals

Lab work and projects are part of the grade, not an afterthought — from science experiments to computer programs.

📖 Reading culture

Library periods are built into the timetable. Every student is expected to read beyond the textbook.

👩‍🏫 Small class sizes

No section exceeds 35 students. Most primary sections have 25–30, so teachers can give individual attention.
